# Goal: a bug report (the bug report will be written in {language})

{include:parts/bug-report.md}

## 8. The reporter's words - the report never mentions the recording

The readers of this report do not get the recording (a client, for example). The report
must read as if the bug had been described to you, not recorded:

- `bugreport.md`, the captions of the screenshots and the line in `bugs/README.md` never
  mention the recording, a video, a narration or a transcript, never give a time stamp
  of the recording, and never name its folder or the day it was made.
- Describe in your own words what I (the reporter) point out, claim or expect, and what I
  did - for example "The reporter points out that the Technical tab offers a table of bids;
  in their view it should offer only the technical group ID." Do not quote me and do not
  retell my sentences one by one: describe what I meant.
- **Notes** never say what the report is based on. A side observation is phrased without
  the recording, for example "Side observation, not raised by the reporter: ...".
- The folder of the recording and the time range go only into your chat message to me
  (section 7).
