# Goal: a bug report with video (the bug report will be written in {language})

{include:parts/bug-report.md}

## 8. The reporter's words and the recording

The readers of this report also get the recording (our testers, for example), so the
report may point into it:

- You may say what I (the reporter) said or showed and where in the recording, with the
  time stamp of the recording (seconds or m:ss).
- Quote me when my words matter, but always in the language of the report: translate the
  quote, never keep the language I spoke. Mark a quote as mine and give its time stamp.
- **Notes** name the folder of the recording and the time range where the bug is shown.
